Quick Information

Access & Intended Users

  • Access: Restricted to authenticated scientific staff
  • Group: Department of Optical and Rocket Soundings
  • Institute: Leibniz Institute of Atmospheric Physics (IAP), Kühlungsborn
  • Scope: Internal model and input/forcing files (not publicly accessible)

What Makes MIMAS Valuable

  • Explicit coupling of transport + microphysics in a 3-D framework
  • Hybrid representation: Eulerian H₂O with a Lagrangian particle ensemble
  • Designed for controlled experiments on mixing (Kz), transport, and ice evolution
  • Structured to support traceability from code → physics → literature
