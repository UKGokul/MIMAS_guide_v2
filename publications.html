MIMAS-family model studies & applications

Selected applications (1): local time dependence, vortex influence, hydroxyl trends, and solar-cycle effects

Authors: Schmidt, F., Baumgarten, G., Berger, U., Fiedler, J., & Lübken, F.-J. (2018); Grygalashvyly, M. et al. (2020, 2021); Lübken, F.-J., Berger, U., & Baumgarten, G. (2009)

Journal: Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics; Annales Geophysicae; Journal of Atmospheric and Solar-Terrestrial Physics; Journal of Geophysical Research

DOI: (add DOI links)

What this paper contributes to MIMAS

  • Demonstrates model capability to reproduce local time dependence of PMCs/NLCs (model study).
  • Shows how middle-atmosphere dynamics (e.g., polar vortex) can impact composition in model frameworks.
  • Provides examples of long-term / climatological applications using MIMAS-family infrastructure.

Selected applications (2): anthropogenic impact, mesospheric temperature trends, and greenhouse-gas effects on solar-cycle response

Authors: Lübken, F.-J., Berger, U., & Baumgarten, G. (2018a, 2018b); Vellalassery, A., Baumgarten, G., Grygalashvyly, M., & Lübken, F.-J. (2023)

Journal: Geophysical Research Letters; Journal of Geophysical Research; Annales Geophysicae

DOI: (add DOI links)

What this paper contributes to MIMAS

  • Establishes how the framework is used for long-term evolution and trend studies of mesospheric ice/NLC.
  • Provides context for interpreting modeled temperature and NLC trends in a climate-change setting.
  • Connects greenhouse-gas forcing to solar-cycle responses in water vapor and NLCs in a model-based study.
